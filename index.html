Can LLM agents beat Nash without talking?

Centre for Intelligent Machines, McGill

Built a benchmark of one-shot, no-communication matrix games to test whether LLM agents can exceed the Nash baseline through self-play reasoning alone. Frontier models consistently beat Nash in two-player games, but coordination degraded as team size and action space grew.
